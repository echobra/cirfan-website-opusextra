cirfan does not guarantee success, sales, revenue, or any specific advertising performance from the creatives we produce.

## Why

Ad results depend on many things outside our control, including your product, offer, pricing, landing page, targeting, budget, and each platform’s delivery systems.

## What we do instead

We ask for your best-performing creatives and their lifetime metrics, and optionally a few competitor references. New creatives are built around the styles, hooks, and calls to action that have already worked for you. This informs our work. It is not a promise of results.
