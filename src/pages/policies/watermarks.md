cirfan may add watermarks to generated media to prevent unauthorized use before payment.

## Removal

After the client is satisfied with the media and their payment has been successful, cirfan removes the watermark. Removal automatically grants the client commercial use of the delivered media. Ownership transfers as described in the [Intellectual property ownership policy](/policies/ip-ownership).

## Watermarked drafts

Watermarked media is for review only. It may not be published, used commercially, or edited to remove or hide the watermark.

## Demos on this website

The demo creatives on this website carry a moving watermark to discourage use, abuse, replication, or duplication. They may not be downloaded, reused, or reproduced.
