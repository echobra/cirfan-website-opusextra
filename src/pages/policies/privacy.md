This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

## What is stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard technical data, such as IP addresses in server logs, to deliver and secure the site.

## Email

If you email cirfan, your message and contact details are used only to reply and to carry out any project you start. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

## Person in charge

Edward, owner of cirfan, is responsible for the protection of personal information.
